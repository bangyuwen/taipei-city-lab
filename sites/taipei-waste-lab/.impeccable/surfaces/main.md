# Taipei Waste Lab redesign

Scope: this standalone site only. Mode: Operate for map, comparison and design; Read for research.
Audience: residents and researchers reading Traditional Chinese on phones and desktops.
User authorizes a coherent redesign and direct implementation; this direction is selected within that authority.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A public research index connects a saved location to its source, comparison and service implications. The first screen starts the task instead of a large declaration.
OWN-WORLD: Library open-shelf indexing lends deep petrol navigation, cool white pages, pale blue selections, tabular data and a precise selected-row marker. Standard web controls remain familiar.
STORY: Choose a version, read its limits, find a location, then inspect a comparison or evidence. Country cases and research keep cost, result and local interpretation distinct.
FIRST VIEWPORT: A 224px left navigation rail, compact two-line introduction, history subnavigation, four-column version strip, and a filter/list column next to the map. On phones the rail becomes an expandable menu and filters precede map and results.
FORM: Grounded candidate 6, public library open-shelf index. Seed e6fca0a4, assigned index 6. Other candidates considered: public information signage, municipal statistical yearbook, river wayfinding, transit service notices, museum collection labels, and community notice boards. The chosen library system supplies type, palette, density and selected-entry emphasis only.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
