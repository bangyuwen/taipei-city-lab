# T1 Taipei street bin design proposal

Status: concept for Taipei pedestrian streets and shopping districts, 2026-10-16. No fabricated prototype, supplier quote, physical acceptance result or engineering sign-off exists. The public proposal lives in the design route of `dist/index.html`; this document records its product and validation boundary.

## Intended service and geometry

T1 accepts small carried general litter such as used tissues. Bottles go to recycling; household bags and business waste require their own authorized collection. If a site needs recycling, provide a separately labeled and collected companion bin and assess the combined footprint. The other three interactive purposes are separate concepts, not T1 configurations.

| Item | Proposed target | Still to establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Closed outer envelope | 480 W × 420 D × 1100 H mm | Tolerances, edge radii, actual projections |
| Front opening | 220 W × 143 H mm, center 825 mm above ground | Mock-up reach, obstruction depth, animal access and weather performance |
| Liner | 60 L useful volume target; open top, liquid-retaining bottom, wheeled front removal | Commercial fit, usable volume, rated load, full wet mass, wheels, bag compatibility |
| Outer panels | Replaceable 304 stainless steel, proposed 1.2–1.5 mm, fine matte finish | Material certificates, frame/folds, corrosion finish, impact and structural performance |
| User operation | Recessed intake with hood and offset baffle; no user flap or pedal | Rain, odor and animal-access acceptance |
| Service operation | Unlock and secure front door, roll liner out, exchange bag, clean, return, lock | Pull force, threshold transition, safe work zone and vehicle transfer |

The rain cap slopes rearward within the closed envelope. There is no residual-liquid pouring port and no liquid drain to the street or stormwater system. The liner catches incidental leakage. Washing takes place at a service location that collects wastewater. The model does not resolve sheet joins, structural members, fabrication tolerances or a rated payload. Its visible thicknesses, liner volume and wheel travel are explanatory geometry.

Use the site's petrol accent around the intake, pale body panels, a drawn litter pictogram and bilingual text. Include a replaceable asset number and genuine management contact when deployed. Do not rely on color alone or display a fake operational QR code. The manual base proposal excludes cameras, payment, resident identification, solar and compaction equipment.

## Siting and operations

Use at least 1500 mm continuous unobstructed walking width after installation as the planning target; on-site review establishes applicable requirements. A 2000 mm sidewalk minus 420 mm bin depth minus a proposed 50 mm setback leaves 1530 mm before all other obstacles. This is an estimate, not an approval. The interactive readout deliberately uses sidewalk minus bin depth only and labels its omissions.

Initially reserve about 800 mm in front for the service operation, inside a setback or widened area that retains the continuous walking route. Validate this against the actual door, liner and worker. Reject sites where the operating envelope cannot fit. Avoid tactile guidance, entrances, sight lines and fire equipment. Anchorage, substrate, underground utilities, wind/impact/tip resistance and foundation details require site-specific professional decisions; no bolt size or load rating is prescribed here.

An assigned operator owns inspection and collection, increased visits for overflow, spill response, lockout and repairs. Record asset ID, date/time, fill level, waste characteristics, duration, incidents and repair result. Do not invent a fixed current municipal cleaning frequency. Prevent unsafe sharp edges, pinch points, unexpected door closing and unintended liner movement through prototype development.

## Replacement groups and quotation

Replaceable groups: intake insert; roof; outer panels; door hinges and lock; liner and wheel assembly; labels; mounting base. Validate common tools, spare availability, material isolation, bag fit and safe disassembly. Obtain separate quotes for design/tooling, shell, liner/wheels, labels, foundation, transport/installation, spare parts, warranty and annual operating costs. Prices, production quantity, warranty term and replacement intervals remain unknown. Neither overseas equipment prices nor the site's unrelated 30-site pilot budget estimates T1 cost.

## Prototype and acceptance plan

1. Build a full-size inexpensive mock-up; assess standing/seated reach, opening depth, leg/foot approach, visibility and service envelope with wheelchair users, older pedestrians and actual collection workers.
2. Agree test methods, loads, quantitative acceptance limits, owner and independent sign-off needs before making and testing the metal prototype.
3. Test rain from agreed directions/intensity/duration, retained-liquid leakage, cleaning, odor containment and animal access. An open intake is not proof that these requirements are met.
4. Measure pull force and removal time at the agreed maximum service mass; inspect threshold crossing, bag damage, wheel behavior, door restraint, sharp edges and pinch points. Establish vehicle-transfer compatibility.
5. Have appropriate specialists establish and verify corrosion, operating-cycle, impact, stability and anchoring criteria. Correct failed items before installation.
6. After approval, run a small documented pilot with a baseline. Track overflow, missorting, leakage, labor, maintenance and complaints at comparable times, recording weather and pedestrian demand. Treat before/after observations descriptively. Stop use for leaking, failed attachment, dangerous pinch points or blocked access.

The design is complete as a concept brief. Production drawings, engineering approval, quotations and physical tests remain required downstream work, not results implied by the website.

## Evidence and limits

Sources checked 2026-10-16. Proposed numerical dimensions above are authored choices, not statutory bin dimensions.

- [General waste recycling, removal and disposal regulation, articles 11–12](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?PCODE=O0050024): outdoor containers must address leakage, odor, rain and animal access; recycling containers need prescribed identifying information. These are prototype acceptance considerations, not verified T1 performance.
- [Taipei DEP placement principles](https://www.dep.gov.taipei/News_Content.aspx?n=ACEFA960B5A4ACD7&s=B90A10FBBF73521B): sidewalk width and pedestrian/traffic assessment guide placement. The FAQ's sidewalk-width language alone does not establish residual clear width.
- [Urban roads and ancillary works design standard, article 16](https://laws.gov.taipei/Law/LawSearch/LawArticleContent/FL037831): general pedestrian clear-width requirement and conditional exceptions. This proposal does not grant an exception.
- [Taiwan accessible building design guideline, appendix A102](https://www.nlma.gov.tw/uploads/files/e9abb44ff171074d23a7d1d5ee35b4a4.pdf): reference reach guidance; not street-bin certification. Obstructions and approach affect reach.
- [Taipei DEP cup recycling guidance](https://www.dep.gov.taipei/News_Content.aspx?n=D6521D207DE817C2&s=83534F468DAF3FF0&sms=53780C1906F94AB4): empty beverage cups can use obligated retail collection channels. This does not authorize street disposal of residual liquids.
- [Household waste exclusion](https://www.dep.gov.taipei/News_Content.aspx?n=ACEFA960B5A4ACD7&s=84EC6A2E88CC22ED&sms=87415A8B9CE81B16) and [overflow reporting/collection](https://www.dep.gov.taipei/News_Content.aspx?n=ACEFA960B5A4ACD7&s=B58711D230F0D78B&sms=87415A8B9CE81B16).
- [Historical Taipei bin maintenance SOP FL076512](https://laws.gov.taipei/Law/LawSearch/LawArticleContent/FL076512) stopped applying on 2022-08-24. Its old material/spacing/cleaning rules are not presented as current requirements.
